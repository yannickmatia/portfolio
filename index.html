Applied thinking

When lead qualification drops, where do you look first?

For an interview exercise, I traced a hypothetical drop in lead-to-MQL conversion through lifecycle handoffs, lead scoring and routing, nurture design, and measurement. The goal was not to rush toward one answer. It was to ask better questions in the right order.

A proposed approach, not an implemented client project or a claim of achieved conversion improvement.
