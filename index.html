APPLIED THINKING · INTERVIEW EXERCISE

When lead qualification drops, where do you look first?

In a hypothetical HubSpot and Salesforce environment, lead-to-MQL conversion fell from 25% to 18%, nurture programs were fragmented, and Sales questioned lead quality. My assessment proposed the following approach.

  • Trace the journey. Review 30–50 recent leads to identify failure patterns across lifecycle handoffs, scoring definitions, and attribution.
  • Structure the nurture. Separate webinar registrants, attendees, and no-shows, with confirmation, reminder, recording, and follow-up messages appropriate to each group.
  • Connect qualification to routing. Combine demographic fit and behavioral signals; route qualified leads by score, geography, and industry, with context for the sales representative.
  • Measure the handoff. Assess lead qualification, sales acceptance, scoring accuracy, and program performance across regions.

This is a proposed approach from an interview assessment, not an implemented client project or a claim of achieved conversion improvement.
