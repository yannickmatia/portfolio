About Yannick Matia

Curious by nature.
Practical by necessity.

Yannick Matia is a Brooklyn-based marketing operations practitioner and writer. His work spans CRM, automation, campaign operations, analytics, and the human habits that make those systems useful.

He studied Political Economy and Environmental Studies at the University of Southern California. That interest in systems never really left. It simply found new places to show up.

Beyond work, Yannick writes, runs, takes photographs, studies history, and spends an entirely reasonable amount of emotional energy on the Lakers and Paris Saint-Germain.
